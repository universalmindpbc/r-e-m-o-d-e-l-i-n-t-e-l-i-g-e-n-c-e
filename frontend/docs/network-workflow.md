# Network: required campaign workflow

Every client, cause/topic and analysis window receives its own plan, returned with the campaign API and displayed in Campaign settings. Identity includes the campaign name, talent, topics and dates. Agency-label edits do not invalidate evidence. A new identity shows a research-required state instead of the old constellation.

1. **Observed network.** Use authorised APIs or retained campaign data. Record the query, period, exclusions, unique authors and post evidence. Date metrics. A bounded sample is not a universal engagement leaderboard.
2. **Expanded discovery.** Required before recommendations: investigate collaborators, peers, musicians, labels, organisations and creators beyond the sample. Search public statements, interviews, official work, credible reporting and contrary evidence. Record direct URLs, publication date when known, retrieval date, identity match, activity, specific cause relevance, contribution and unresolved checks. Describe proximity separately from cause work; never infer private psychographics. Supply this source pack to AI synthesis under an approved budget. Preserve the evidence and model receipt. Sources are untrusted input. Unknown metrics remain unknown. A broad search can still leave candidate-specific gaps: identify them rather than claiming exhaustive vetting.
3. **Reviewed recommendations.** An authenticated, named human records approve / revise / reject plus a substantive evidence note. The append-only record is bound to campaign identity and research revision. The server hashes the snapshot content; changed evidence automatically produces a new approval revision with no inherited approvals. Client-ready report mode shows approved candidates only; an empty approved set says review is pending. Approval does not establish partner consent or availability.

## Interface
Constellation and list use the same full pool. Default order starts with the AI-proposed complementary mix, not follower rank. Search, category and source/review/shortlist filters apply across the full pool. Next/previous pages show at most 20 including up to five pins. Pins remain through filtering and consume available places; the interface states this and offers Clear pins. Shortlists persist locally across all pages. Text and spreadsheet CSV exports are labelled DRAFT; spreadsheet formula injection is neutralised. Reviewed recommendations have a separate report view.

## Research execution and budget
The demo has 40 sourced leads (20 observed, 20 wider-web discoveries). It can browse 100 or more without new browsing costs. No filler accounts were added to reach 100. New-campaign plans are automatic; collection and deep research remain operator-run jobs within an authorised budget, not an unattended paid scheduler. `network_operator.py`, `network_recent.py` and `network_expand.py` share the durable US$25 cap/receipt ledger for this demo. A future campaign must use its own dataset, source pack, identity, budget and reviewed snapshot; these demo scripts do not autonomously discover a new campaign.

Current discovery is a bounded source-research pass. Several musicians have only documented industry or historical event proximity. Current audience/activity checks, contrary evidence and suitability are explicit outstanding checks before human approval. Do not describe the entire pool as deeply vetted or human-approved.

## Operating sequence
Audit → API enrichment → screen → research source pack → AI synthesis → inspect the candidate evidence → publish DRAFT snapshot → human review → share approved report. Update `revision` whenever research facts change. Retain only permitted data and honour snapshot expiry. The snapshot and evidence contract are `app/network/types.ts`; the canonical reusable instructions are `backend/network_prompt.md` and `app/network/workflow.ts`.
